Walther PPK/S for Project Zomboid

Forge session, 5 November 2026 · Goal: turn the idea into concrete decisions · Outcome: hardened and ready for a spec

Locked decisions

  • LockBuild 42 only; no B41 version.
  • LockPPK/S in .380 ACP with a 7-round magazine. Gun and mod are named “Walther PPK/S”.
  • LockNew .380 round, box and magazine, under the mod’s own name so later .380 guns can share them.
  • LockCommon in civilian spots (homes, gun stores, cars, safes), never police or military; .380 scarcer than 9mm.
  • LockIts only edge over the M9 is low weight; noise and everything else stay realistic.
  • Lock3D model from a free source whose license allows changes and sharing.
  • LockDisplay names only in translation files, never in item IDs.
  • LockLean version 1: no attachments, vanilla pistol sounds.

Rejected, and why

  • KilledReusing 9mm: unrealistic, and it turns the gun into a reskinned M9.
  • Killed.32 ACP: same work as .380, weaker, and rarer in the US.
  • KilledMaking it quieter: a real .380 isn’t meaningfully quieter.
  • KilledA generic vanilla-style name: works against the realism direction.
  • KilledThe classic 6-round PPK: weaker, and less true to 1993 Kentucky.
  • KilledAttachments in version 1: no rail on a PPK/S, and extra work before the first release.

Weak points that survived

  • RiskThe weight edge may be too small to feel; players may swap it out early. Check in playtesting.
  • RiskThe model’s license must be confirmed, and its polygon count and texture size cut to match vanilla guns.
  • RiskSmall trademark risk from the real name; a rename only touches translations and the Workshop page.
  • OpenThe mod ID and item namespace aren’t chosen yet, and can’t contain “/”.
