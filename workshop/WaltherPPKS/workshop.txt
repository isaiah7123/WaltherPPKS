description=[h2]Adds[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Walther PPK/S (.380 ACP, 7-round magazine). Lighter than the M9 (1.0) with M1911-level noise (radius 70); damage 0.6-1.0, range 10; everything else follows the vanilla M9. Vanilla pistol sounds, no attachments.
description=[*] .380 ACP rounds, boxes of 50 (own box art) and PPK/S magazines. Open a box into 50 rounds, pack 50 rounds into a box. No carton.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Loot[/h2]
description=Civilian loot only (homes, gun stores, pawn shops, cars, stashes): the PPK/S spawns at 75% of the M9's chance and every .380 item at half the matching 9mm chance. Never in police, military, SWAT, security, prison-guard or sheriff loot, nor on zombies. Every PPK/S spawns with a magazine.
description=
description=[h2]For modders[/h2]
